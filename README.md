# Bias in LLMs
### Exploring Bias in Large Language Model-Generated Stories

**Natural Language Processing · Responsible AI · LLM Evaluation**

> When an AI tells a story, what assumptions shape the characters, roles, and possibilities it imagines?

## Overview

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used to create stories, educational content, and other personalized experiences. Although these stories may appear neutral, subtle patterns in character portrayals, personality traits, and social roles can reflect familiar stereotypes.

This is a research project investigating how these patterns emerge in LLM-generated narratives, with a particular focus on stories written for children. The project aims to better understand the ways narrative framing and generation context can influence representation and what this means for developing more responsible generative AI systems.

## What We Explore

- **Gender representation:** How stories may differ in the qualities, opportunities, and roles associated with different recipients and characters.
- **Narrative perspective:** Whether the storyteller's role influences how people and relationships are portrayed.
- **Cultural context:** How storytelling contexts may shape recurring themes and social expectations.
- **Consistency across models:** Whether similar patterns appear across different LLMs.

## Research Highlights

Our analyses examine subtle differences in how generated stories emphasize **initiative, independence, care, relationships, and social roles**. The broader goal is not simply to identify biased words, but to understand how patterns of representation may arise in otherwise engaging and well-written narratives.

This work contributes to ongoing discussions about **fairness, representation, and the responsible use of generative AI in child-facing applications**.
